Calibration capture

Record yourself signing letters, so a model can be trained on your own hand. Everything stays in this tab until you press Download.
idle
How to record. Pick a letter, form it, press Record, and hold still for the countdown. Then lower your hand completely, raise it, re-form the letter, and record again. Do 5–6 separate takes per letter, moving slightly between them — a little closer or further, a little higher, turned a few degrees.

Re-forming the pose between takes is the whole point. Six separate takes are worth far more than one long one, because frames inside a single take are near-duplicates and count as one example, not many.
