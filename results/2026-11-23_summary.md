# Summary of the 22–23 Nov changes (for the team and supervisor)

**Scope.** The 30 static PSL letters. The 11 letters signed with movement (آ ؤ ئ ج ش م ن ٹ ڑ ھ ہ)
are out of scope and listed as a limitation.

**How accuracy is measured now.** Only on correctly labelled pictures of people the model was
never trained on. Earlier figures (26–40%) were scored against the letter-sheet labels, about half
of which are wrong, so they are not comparable with the figures below.

## Final models

| model | trained on | accuracy on people it never saw |
|---|---|---|
| **Public** (website, in git) | our calibration takes (291) + the letter-sheet pictures that passed the label check (517 of 1,028) | **70.8%** on Kamran's image set (2,322 images, several signers) |
| same, with all letter sheets (before) | our takes + all 1,028 sheet pictures | 67.8% |
| letter sheets only | 1,028 sheet pictures | 52.6% |
| **Private** (lab machines only, not in git) | Kamran's images (2,322) + our takes (291), no letter sheets | **64.3%** on our takes, with Kamran's images alone as training* |

\* Anyone in a model's training cannot be its test. The private model's own figure is estimated by
training on Kamran's images alone and testing on our two people. With all letter sheets added it
falls to 57.7%. Live, informally (23 Nov): it read Abdullah's static letters well both with and
without his own takes in training.

All figures: SVM (the model type the website runs), 30 static letters.
`clean_eval.py`, `cleaned_eval.py`, `export_geometry_model.py [--clean-sheets | --with-kamran --no-video]`.

## What changed, and why

1. **Letter-sheet labels: about half are wrong.** The sheets are frames cut automatically from word
   videos, and the cut often lands on a neighbouring letter (e.g. a م picture showing ل). A model
   trained only on correct pictures flagged 48% of sheet pictures; in 46% of those its guess was the
   neighbouring letter of the word. **A blind human review confirmed it: 85% of flagged pictures
   were wrong, against 20% of kept ones** (40 + 40 pictures, p = 1×10⁻⁸).
2. **Kamran's image set: the detector had found the wrong hand in 46% of images** (the idle hand at
   the signer's side). Re-detected with two hands, keeping the signing one: 1,316 images recovered,
   2,322 usable static images.
3. **What the letter sheets are worth.** Only extra people: with just our two people as clean
   training, the cleaned sheets add 14.5 points; once Kamran's set is in training, they add nothing
   (63.2% with cleaned sheets vs 64.3% without).
4. **ExtraTrees**, a different model type, scores 5–7 points higher on the same tests (74.7% on
   Kamran's images; 69.8% on our takes). Not yet in the website.

## Limits

- Kamran's images are clean studio photos; a live webcam is likely harder.
- Our calibration data is two people, recorded in one sitting; no different-day figure yet.
- Words were not re-measured with these models (left for after letters).
- Using Kamran's images in the public model needs his permission (the model file contains training rows).
